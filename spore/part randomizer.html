Gives you a random (vanilla) creature part or paint from any category. You can use this to make a creature with random parts!
Part icons are reused from my all parts pages.

All of these are achievable with vanilla (I even added Maxis cells or creatures you can pick from for most cell paints), but some parts are easier to use with mods and otherwise would require some planning (editing an early creature or the Grox to get their parts).

It might roll the same part or paint again if it fails a check, you can just click the button again to get a different part if that's an issue.

Generating random colors and hues is a lot more difficult to code. You can generate a random hex color here, and then, if you don't have Enhanced Color Picker, run it through the color matcher and use that.

If you want, you can also generate random numbers here (with 2 to 20 you can randomize the amount of vertebrae on your creature, and you can randomize the amount of other parts, for example)



 
(The numbers are inclusive, but actually become exclusive if you have the larger one in min and the smaller one in max)
