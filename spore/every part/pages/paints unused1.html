Unused skinpaints

Colors, used paints and descriptions come from the skinPaint_script~ files. Colors shown are as converted from floats in SporeModder FX, and might be ever so slightly off from their actual ingame color
Technically flora paints are all unused but that doesn't count, these are the ones that are fully inaccessible in vanilla, even with the -state:FloraEditor launch command
With one exception (fe_skinpaint_floracoat_platesLarge), none of them have associated effect files, so they will not work even if implemented with a mod (without creating an effect) or Cheat Engine
