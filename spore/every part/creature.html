Model capabilities determine various stats, as well as abilities that the part gives. Charm is a model capability, so is health, so is any model capability that seems to just exist to define certain animations for the part

Cuteness and meanness values exist for most creature parts, and I think they do seem to be used, like in SpeciesArchetypes (herdtypes~)

Parts that ignore skinpaint particles don't get affected by skinpaint brushes, so, for example, on the eggshell base paint (see the paints page to see which one it is), they will be the opposite color from the set base color

Models not being jigglable, or being extra jiggly, just determines how much it will jiggle around as the creature animates
Mouth type determines the used voice for a mouth, and mouth priority determines the sapient creature mouth voice used on creature with multiple mouths (with different sapient mouth sounds). Mammal voices have a higher priority than bird mouths, and then insect mouths

Eye overlay is unused in retail Spore, same for the day and night vision values, but most eyes still retain them

Limb block counts is just the amount of parts (block) that they use, each limb segment is a part

I'm not sure what modelCapabilitySocial is!! Some parts with social stats don't have it, but some graspers that DON'T have social stats do have it

Foot type determines the sound used for footsteps. Feet with an undefined foot type just won't make any footstep sounds
