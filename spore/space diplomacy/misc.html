These are referenced from both locale/en-us/Text.package - locale~/space_diplomacy~.locale, and Spore_Game.package\space_conversations~\space_diplomacy~.cnv, as well as other files from this folder
The line breaks in the original locales were just "~p~", I retained the ones that were present at the end of a dialogue entry, which is why some will have empty line breaks.
I've retained some values and actions that I can't make sense of, but might be useful? However I excluded all the eventlog strings from the mission and event localization files.
I also kept the dialogue order the same for both the sets of dialogues and also the lines within them. If the order seems weird, that's on the original .cnv files.
