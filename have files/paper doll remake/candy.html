Known issue with outfit codes not generating

I found, at least on desktop Firefox, if you close the tab and reopen it with "reopen closed tab", generating outfit codes doesn't update the output box anymore. Refreshing the page also doesn't help, and to fix it, you have either Ctrl+F5, or click on the address bar and press Enter to reset the page. No clue what the problem is???

If this happens to you and you don't want to reset the page and lose your outfit, use inspect element, and click on the Console tab. The correct outfit code will be logged there. If you're on mobile, just look up how to open inspect element for your browser, I guess.

this is a Space Goofs fan project, based on the original flash game by Xilam (redrawn with more clothing options and recolorability). the original game is available on flashpoint
art & coding by zelenbug. not affiliated with xilam in any way

the color sliders actually modify an image with filters, and it can be difficult to get a color right, so try fiddling around with the sliders. even tweaking the hue on something with 0 brightness and 400 saturation (like if you want white clothes) will affect its brightness!

you can click on the value of a color slider and type it in manually to set it more precisely as well. the color will update once you click away from the input box

the colors will reset if you switch to a different outfit piece for the layer, so be warned!!

some of the clothes are based on the original game, and go together in sets here as well, but it should be easier to mix them together now (with the ability to actually recolor clothes)
